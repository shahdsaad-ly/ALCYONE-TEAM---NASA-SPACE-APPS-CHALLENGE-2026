
## ALCYONE TEAM PRESENTS
**The Machines NASA Left Behind**

A multi-page educational website for school-age space enthusiasts, created by **Team ALCYONE** for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

## The Challenge

Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system: on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers and instruments that completed their science missions to probes still traveling away from Earth, this hardware varies widely in both its purpose and its current level of functionality.

Our goal is to tell the story of this equipment in a way that introduces school-age space enthusiasts to the hardware and the science it made possible.

## Our Solution

**Alcyone team presents** an interactive story website that asks one question: *which machines are still working, and which went silent?*

Each page introduces two pieces of NASA hardware, with a card for each one:

| Page | Place | Hardware |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Moon | Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV), Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package (ALSEP) |
| 2 | Mars | Perseverance, Curiosity |
| 3 | Deep Space | Webb's First Deep Field (James Webb Space Telescope), Voyager 1 |

Every card shows:

- an image with its NASA credit
- mission type, location, launch/deployment date and mission duration
- **current status** (completed, still active, or contact lost)
- a short description written for young readers
- scientific achievements
- a "Did you know?" fact
- a link to the NASA source page
- an audio narration player

## Features

- Responsive layout (cards sit side by side on desktop and stack on mobile)
- Spinning Earth on the home page
- Native audio narration for every card
- Previous / next navigation between exploration pages
- Built with plain **HTML5 and CSS3** (no frameworks, no JavaScript required)
