ABOUT THE CHALLENGE

Since the 1960s, NASA has left hardware across the solar system—on the Moon, on Mars, and in deep space. From rovers and instruments that completed their science missions to probes still traveling away from Earth, this equipment varies widely in both its purpose and current level of functionality.

Our challenge is to tell the stories of these remarkable machines, introducing young space enthusiasts to the hardware and the science it made possible.
